## 1. Overview

**Oscillator 2** uses the **exact same 36-slot layout** as [**OSC 1**](02-osc1.md):
- **Slot 1 (Control `1`):** `OSC2 TYPE` (`0..8`, 9 Z1 synthesis models, `ID 188`, `ExID = 0`).
- **Slots 2..36 (Controls `102..136` Faders / `202..236` Lists):** Dynamically reconfigured to expose the parameters of the selected OSC2 synthesis model (`ExID = 2`, `id14 = (2 << 11) | ID = 4096 + ID`).

---

## 2. Supported OSC 2 Models (`ID 188`, `ExID = 0`)

| Value | Model Name | `PROG_PRM.TXT` IDs (`ExID = 2`) | Active Params | Notes |
|:---:|---|---|:---:|---|
| `0` | **Standard OSC** | `464..479` | 16 | Saw/Pulse wave morph, Edge, Sub-style PWM & modulation. |
| `1` | **Comb Filter OSC** | `480..492` | 13 | Impulse/Noise/OSC1 excitation into resonant comb filter. |
| `2` | **VPM OSC** | `493..514` | 22 | Variable Phase Modulation with carrier/modulator harmonics. |
| `3` | **Resonance OSC** | `515..539` | 25 | 4 formant bandpass filters (`Freq 1..4`) + resonance mod. |
| `4` | **Ring Modulation** | `540..553` | 14 | Ring-modulated carrier (can modulate against OSC1). |
| `5` | **Cross Modulation** | `554..567` | 14 | Frequency cross-modulation (can be driven by OSC1). |
| `6` | **Sync OSC** | `568..581` | 14 | Hard sync slave oscillator with dedicated Slave Pitch & Mod. |
| `7` | **Organ Model** | `582..608` | 21 | 3 drawbars (`Harm1..3`) + Percussion generator. |
| `8` | **E.Piano Model** | `588..615` (`ExID 2`) | 13 | Tine/Hammer physical model parameters for OSC2. |
