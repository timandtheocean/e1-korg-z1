# Page 5: Oscillator 2 (`OSC2`)

[← Back to the manual index](https://github.com/timandtheocean/e1-korg-z1/tree/main/usermanual/README.md) · [All manual pages](https://github.com/timandtheocean/e1-korg-z1/tree/main/usermanual/pages)

Oscillator 2 provides a second independent MOSS synthesis voice with **9 synthesis algorithms**. It can act as a rich layered voice alongside Oscillator 1, a dual physical-modeling partner (for example, pairing a Reed woodwind with a Brass horn or Organ), or a modulation/carrier source for Oscillator 1's Ring Mod, Cross Mod, Comb Filter, and Sync models.

Just like the OSC 1 page, the **OSC 2** page **automatically morphs its controls, labels, ranges, and visibility** whenever you change **OSC2 Model**, and uses **thin horizontal faders** for all modulation intensity (`Int`) controls.

| Slot | Control | Description |

| **Col 1** | **OSC2 Model** | Selects which of the 9 synthesis models Oscillator 2 uses. Automatically reconfigures Rows 2–6 and updates the page title. |

| **Col 6** | **Jump OSC 1** | Jumps directly to the [OSC 1](https://github.com/timandtheocean/e1-korg-z1/tree/main/usermanual/pages/02-osc1.md) page. |

## The 9 Synthesis Models of Oscillator 2

Oscillator 2 supports the first 8 models of Oscillator 1 plus the **Reed Model** physical woodwind engine:

1. **Standard OSC (`Standard`)**: Saw and Pulse waveforms with variable Wave Edge symmetry, PWM (`Level` + `Mod1` + `LFO1`), and sub-harmonic `Bottom` warmth.
2. **Comb Filter (`Comb Filter`)**: Tuned delay-line resonator driven by an impulse, noise, OSC 1, Sub OSC, or Noise Generator, with Feedback, High-Frequency Damp, and fine Key Tracking.
3. **Variable Phase Modulation (`VPM`)**: Complete 2-operator phase-modulation synth with Carrier & Modulator waveforms, Coarse/Fine ratio, Feedback, Wave Edge folding, and its own internal multi-stage Modulator Envelope.
4. **Resonance OSC (`Resonance`)**: Saw/Pulse exciter routed through 4 tunable parallel formant filters with collective Cutoff, Frequency Width, Bandwidth, and individual Band 1–4 frequencies and levels.
5. **Ring Modulation (`Ring Mod`)**: Multiplies OSC 2 with **OSC 1**, **Sub OSC**, or **Noise** with controllable Ring Depth, Depth Modulation, and Low Boost.
6. **Cross Modulation (`Cross Mod`)**: Frequency/phase-modulates OSC 2 using **OSC 1**, **Sub OSC**, or **Noise** with Mod1 and LFO1 depth modulation.
7. **Oscillator Sync (`Sync`)**: Hard-syncs OSC 2 to the pitch of **OSC 1** or **Sub OSC** with variable Sync Timbre and Bottom controls.
8. **Organ Model (`Organ Model`)**: 3-drawbar additive organ engine with selectable harmonic ratios, Sine/Triangle waves, individual level modulations, and harmonic percussion click.
9. **Reed Model (`Reed Model`)**: Full physical model of single- and double-reed woodwinds and flutes (`AltoSax`, `Clarinet`, `Oboe`, `Flute`, etc.) with internal Breath Pressure envelope, reed stiffness, bore reflection, growl, and formant shaping.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **OSC 2 is disabled while OSC 1 is a physical model that uses both oscillators.** When **OSC1 Model** is set to **Brass Model**, **Reed Model**, **Plucked String** or **Bowed String**, the Z1 cannot use Oscillator 2. In that case the OSC2 Model widget shows **DISABLED**, the OSC2 page title explains why, all OSC 2 controls on this page and on the OSC Common page are hidden, and changes to the OSC2 model are ignored. Select any other OSC 1 model to bring OSC 2 back. Its previous model and settings are restored.

*(For detailed row-by-row descriptions of each of these 9 models, see [Page 4: Oscillator 1](https://github.com/timandtheocean/e1-korg-z1/tree/main/usermanual/pages/02-osc1.md), as the parameters for all 9 models are identical between OSC 1 and OSC 2.)*

## Sharing Presets Between OSC 1 and OSC 2

Because OSC 1 and OSC 2 share these 9 synthesis models, any per-model oscillator preset you save for one of those 9 models in the **OSC Preset Library** on [Page 3: OSC Common](https://github.com/timandtheocean/e1-korg-z1/tree/main/usermanual/pages/03b-osc-common.md) is **automatically available to both OSC 1 and OSC 2**!

- For example, if you craft a complex 2-operator FM patch using **VPM** on **OSC 1** and save it in the VPM preset list, you can switch **OSC 2** to **VPM** and load that exact preset onto **OSC 2**—or save both oscillators together as a **Both OSCs** dual-oscillator preset.
